# Inkrush

A real-time, interactive multiplayer drawing and guessing game inspired by skribbl.io

Gather your friends, join a room, and put your drawing and deduction skills to the test! In every round, one player is chosen to be the "artist" and selects a word to draw on a shared digital canvas. The rest of the players race against the clock to watch the drawing unfold and type the correct word into the live chat. 

The faster you guess the correct word, the more points you earn—and the artist gets bonus points if people successfully guess their masterpiece. The player with the most points at the end of all the rounds takes the crown!

### Key Features
* **Multiplayer Rooms:** Easily create or join game rooms to play with friends in real-time.
* **Live Interactive Canvas:** Watch the artist's strokes appear synchronously across all players' screens.
* **Real-time Chat & Guessing:** A lively chat box that doubles as the guessing mechanism, automatically detecting correct answers and rewarding points.
* **Word Selection:** Artists get to choose from a curated list of words to draw when it's their turn.
* **Dynamic Scoring & Leaderboard:** Point systems rewarding quick guesses and good drawings, with a live leaderboard showing everyone's current standing.
* **Customizable Rules:** Configurable settings for maximum players, round limits, drawing timers, and point distributions.
